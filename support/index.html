Support

Noiso support for playback, presets, and the sleep timer.

Use this page as the public Support URL for Noiso, the offline sleep sound app for iPhone. It is focused on current playback, background audio, and timer behavior.

Last updated: March 30, 2026

What Noiso Includes

  • Seven bundled ambient presets.
  • Play and pause from the main screen.
  • Sleep timer with infinity, 30m, 1h, and 2h options.
  • Background playback and lock screen controls.

Common Questions

Does Noiso work offline?

Yes. Noiso uses bundled local audio in the iPhone app, so streaming and account setup are not required.

Does Noiso support background playback and lock screen controls?

Yes. The iPhone app supports background audio playback and lock screen controls.

How does the sleep timer work?

Noiso includes infinity, 30 minutes, 1 hour, and 2 hours. Pausing stops the active countdown without changing the selected timer type, and timed playback ends with a soft fade-out.

Does switching presets reset the active timer?

No. Switching presets does not clear the active sleep timer. If playback is active, the new preset starts immediately.
