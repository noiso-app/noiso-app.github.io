Privacy Policy

Noiso respects your privacy.

Noiso is designed to work without accounts, ads, subscriptions, or cloud-based playback. The app uses bundled local audio and does not collect personal information through the app.

Summary

  • No account creation is required.
  • No personal data is collected through the app.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are used.
  • Playback uses bundled local audio files only.
  • Background audio and lock screen controls work entirely on-device.

Information We Collect

Noiso does not collect personal information through the app. The app does not provide registration, messaging, social features, or user-uploaded content.

Local Device Data

Noiso may store a limited local preference on your device, such as the last selected preset, so the app can restore the previous screen state on the next launch. This preference stays on your device unless you remove the app or clear device data.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Noiso does not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking SDKs, or cross-app tracking technologies.

Children's Privacy

Noiso may be used by parents and caregivers as part of baby sleep routines, but the app is intended to be operated by adults. Noiso does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.
